# MRO Domain — Source Library

Full annotated library: `AviOS_MRO_Research_Library.html` at repo root.

## Books (cite, do not reproduce)
- Kinnison & Siddiqui, *Aviation Maintenance Management*, 2nd ed., McGraw-Hill —
  M&E org structure, reliability programs, planning. The user-persona map.
- Lapesa Barrera, *Aircraft Maintenance Programs*, Springer 2022 — EASA vs FAA
  AMP requirements, full MSG-3 methodology. Reference implementation for
  simulator Layers 3–4. https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-030-90263-6
- Moubray, *RCM II*, Industrial Press — RCM practice; with SAE JA1011/JA1012
  (what qualifies as an RCM process).
- Reason & Hobbs, *Managing Maintenance Error* — human factors; error traps,
  procedural drift. Informs AviOS UX so it reduces error opportunity.
- Stolzer et al., *Safety Management Systems in Aviation* — how AviOS risk
  outputs plug into an operator's SMS.
- De Florio, *Airworthiness* — certification/continuing-airworthiness boundary.
- Kroes et al., *Aircraft Maintenance and Repair*, McGraw-Hill — shop-floor
  technical practice (task-card realism).

## Free primary sources
- Nowlan & Heap, *Reliability-Centered Maintenance* (1978):
  https://reliabilitywebfiles.s3.amazonaws.com/Reliability+Centered+Maintenance+by+Nowlan+and+Heap.pdf
- FAA AMT Handbook FAA-H-8083-30 (free, faa.gov) — mechanic curriculum;
  ground-truth vocabulary for task content.
- Skybrary (skybrary.aero) — free encyclopedic reference for operational concepts.
- FAA SDRS (Service Difficulty Reporting System) — av-info.faa.gov — failure
  base rates per ATA chapter.
- NASA ASRS — asrs.arc.nasa.gov — maintenance-related safety narratives.

## Licensed standards (purchase before embedding structure in shipped product)
- MSG-3 Vol 1 & 2 — Airlines for America (A4A)
- ATA iSpec 2200 (technical documentation) — A4A
- ATA Spec 2000 (materiel/provisioning e-commerce) — A4A
- SAE JA1011 / JA1012 (RCM criteria) — SAE International

## Regulatory texts (free, official)
- 14 CFR Parts 43, 121, 145 — ecfr.gov
- FAA AC 120-16 (Air Carrier Maintenance Programs) — faa.gov
- EASA Part-M, Part-CAMO, Part-145 — easa.europa.eu (Easy Access Rules)
