## Market sizing & outlook
- Oliver Wyman, *Global Fleet & MRO Market Forecast 2026–2036*:
  https://www.oliverwyman.com/our-expertise/insights/2026/feb/global-fleet-and-mro-market-forecast-2026-2036.html
  (refreshed annually each February — always pull the latest edition)

  https://www.iata.org/en/pressroom/2025-releases/2025-12-09-02/

## Cost benchmarks
- IATA Maintenance Cost data eXchange (MCX): https://www.iata.org/mcx
  - FY2024 public report: https://www.iata.org/contentassets/bf8ca67c8bcd4358b3d004b0d6d0916f/fy2024-mcx-report_public.pdf
  - FY2023 public report: https://www.iata.org/contentassets/8437020db31a4717b70677d9b06b1a45/fy2023-mcx-report_public.pdf
- IATA Maintenance Cost Technical Group (guidelines, webinars):
  https://www.iata.org/en/programs/ops-infra/techops/mctg/
- US BTS Form 41, Schedule P-5.2 (carrier maintenance cost filings):
  https://www.transtats.bts.gov/
